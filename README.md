# Radiometry Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

Radiometry is an internet radio player for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It does not collect, store, or share personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## Data stored on your device

Your station list, last-played station, volume, and app settings are saved locally on your device. They are never sent to us and are removed when you delete the app.

## Network requests

To work, Radiometry connects to third-party services directly from your device:

- **Radio stations** – audio is streamed from each station's own server.
- **Station directories** – when you search for stations, your search text is sent to Radio Browser (radio-browser.info) and SomaFM.
- **Song identification** – when you identify a song, a short audio sample from the station stream (not your microphone) is converted to an audio signature and matched using Apple's ShazamKit.
- **Apple Music** – if you choose to add a song to a playlist, Radiometry asks for access to your Apple Music library and uses Apple's MusicKit to add it. Access can be revoked at any time in Settings.

These services may receive standard connection information such as your IP address and are governed by their own privacy policies, including [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Microphone

Radiometry does not use your microphone.

## Children

Radiometry does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else.
